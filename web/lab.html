Live Voice Lab

Sing a vowel. Your voice goes through 16 kHz LPC to a 16-section tube, then to the 16-layer film with the same reflection coefficients, then to the colour of that film. The colour is computed live by the same physics as the rest of the project (physics.js, checked against the Python reference).

Records the colour of every voiced 16 ms frame. Silent frames leave dark gaps.

What you are seeing

  1. Capture. Microphone (echo cancellation, noise suppression and AGC are switched off) or a bundled vowel WAV, through an AudioWorklet. A windowed-sinc resampler (anti-aliased at 7.2 kHz) converts to 16 kHz. Frames are 512 samples (32 ms) with a 16 ms hop.
  2. Gate. RMS above an auto-calibrated noise floor (+ margin, with hysteresis), plus a voicing check: normalised autocorrelation peak at 60–500 Hz ≥ 0.45 and zero-crossing rate ≤ 0.3. Unvoiced frames do not change the shape. The lights fade gently instead.
  3. LPC → tube. lpc(frame, 16, 0.97) (Levinson–Durbin) gives reflection coefficients km, and from them the areas Am+1 = Am(1+km)/(1−km) (CONTRACT §3). Smoothing is an exponential average of the log-area ratios 2·atanh(k), which is always a stable filter. It is never applied to raw LPC coefficients. area of a real voice: lossless-tube estimate
  4. Tube → film. The finite glottis m0 is added (Rg = −0.9, illustrative). Then each layer gets ni ∝ Ai (ideal mode), or the indices are compressed into 1.0–2.5 (realizable), or snapped to MgF₂/SiO₂/chitin/Al₂O₃/ZnS/TiO₂ (materials). Every layer has optical thickness 428.27 nm. ideal realizable / materials
  5. Film → colour. T(λ) on 360–830 nm, CIE 1931 2° observer, chosen illuminant. Display brightness is normalised to Y = 50 artistic. Because 0–2.3 kHz maps to 650–750 THz, every vowel comes out violet/lavender. That is a measured result, not a styling choice (CONTRACT §7b). Ideal-mode films transmit very little light (Y ≈ 0.1–0.4 %).
  6. Room. The centre tube is the current frame. The tubes on the walls and ceiling are a history: every ~0.1 s of voicing pushes the current area function and colour into the room. Glow, reflections, the minimum radius and the tube path are artistic.
