Record every phoneme

Read each prompt into the microphone. The recordings are saved on the server (data/user_voice/, with python tools/serve.py or the Streamlit app; online they are kept until the app restarts, so use Download all to keep a copy) and replace the downloaded IPA-chart voices and the synthetic voice in the analysis.

Tips: quiet room, microphone ~20 cm from your mouth, same distance every time. Wait for the red button to pulse, then speak. The bar should be green and fill to about half. If it turns red, you're clipping, so move back a little and record again. Chrome's noise suppression, echo cancelling and auto-gain are switched off because they bend the spectrum.
