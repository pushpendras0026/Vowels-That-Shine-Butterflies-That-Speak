What is exact here?
  • exact wing colour vs angle (Abelès TMM, lossless, non-dispersive, CIE 1931 2°, chosen illuminant); T/R spectra; metamer ΔE00 (re-checked with TMM); voice of a Goupillaud stack at θ = 0 (its characteristic polynomial D(z)).
  • approx voices of non-Goupillaud stacks or at θ ≠ 0 (all-pole LPC fit of T(ν)); presets; material snapping; vowel films (finite glottis, realizable compression); nearest vowel (log F1/F2 distance); broad resonances (B > 600 Hz) shown as envelope peaks.
  • artistic display exposure normalisation (Y→50), wing outline/veins/margins, bending, flapping, flight, the garden.
